What marketing support can you ask a supplier for?

2026-08-15

Hello from MAKENOV. Marketing support is a negotiation item, not a favor. There is a set list of what you can request, and a right time to request each item.

Why would a supplier support your marketing?

Because unsold products hurt the supplier too: a partner stuck with inventory never reorders, and without reorders that market closes. Support is closer to joint investment than charity — frame it as "let\u2019s open this market together", not "please help".

What materials can you get?

ItemBest time to ask
Original product photos and videosFirst discussions
Catalog and detail-image source filesFirst discussions
Local-language materialsContract negotiation
Initial ad-cost sharingContract negotiation
Promo items and samplesFirst order
Product trainingAfter the first order
Trade-show supportAfter sales results

Ask for source files: text baked into images must be rebuilt for localization, at your cost.

How far can cost sharing go?

Initial ad-cost sharing succeeds most often when requested together with an exclusivity contract. Show a plan before naming a number — channels, budget, what you will measure. A request with a plan gets reviewed; one without gets refused.

Training and technical support

Innovative products need explaining; if the sales floor cannot explain it, it will not sell. Ask for training materials, online staff training and a technical contact point — without a defined contact, every field question stops a sale.

When to ask

Before signing is strongest — the supplier wants to secure you as a partner, and items written into the contract need no renegotiation. After signing, ask with results: organized sales data makes additional support reviewable.

FAQ

Do small companies provide support?
Materials and training almost always; cost sharing depends on their situation.

Will asking raise my supply price?
It can — negotiate supply price and support together and calculate total cost.

Must it be in the contract?
Preferably. When the contact person changes, verbal promises are hard to verify.
