Getting started › About MAKENOV

Products and suppliers worldwide,
in one place

MAKENOV is a global B2B innovation platform connecting products from suppliers worldwide with verified buyers. Buyers can browse products, check trade terms, and request quotes from the suppliers they are interested in.

Trade terms are disclosed only to verified buyers

Anyone can view product information. Price, minimum order quantity (MOQ), lead time and supply terms are visible only on accounts that have completed business verification.

Main menus

ProductsBrowse listed products by category and search.
SuppliersCheck supplier information, certifications and listed products.
ArticlesGlobal sourcing and export guides, plus market insights.
WishlistSave products of interest and request quotes for several at once.
My pageManage quote requests, their status, and company information.

Three languages

Product information is provided in Korean, Vietnamese and English. You may also write your enquiry in the language you prefer, and request language support from our team when needed.
