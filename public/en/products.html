FAQ

Is it free to join and use?
Yes. Signing up, browsing products, sending quotation requests and business verification are all free.
Why are prices and MOQs locked?
Price, MOQ, lead time and supply terms are visible only to verified distribution partners. Sign up free and verify your business to unlock them.
How does business verification work?
Vietnam verifies by tax code (MST), Korea by business registration number, and other countries by company email domain. It usually takes about a minute.
Do I deal directly with manufacturers?
Yes. Your inquiry goes straight to the manufacturer with no middleman margins. MAKENOV handles matching and verification.
Can MOQs be negotiated?
It varies by product, but many manufacturers are open to smaller trial orders. State your desired quantity in the quotation request.
Which languages can I use?
Vietnamese, English and Korean are all fine. The MAKENOV team can assist with communication when needed.
