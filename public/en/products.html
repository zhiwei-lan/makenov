FAQ

What is MAKENOV?
MAKENOV is a B2B platform connecting suppliers of innovative global products with distributors in Vietnam. Browse products, request a meeting and talk to the supplier in person. We also support communication and scheduling.
Does it cost anything?
No. Browsing products, requesting meetings and attending events are all free. Supplier introductions, interpretation and meeting coordination are free as well.
How do I request a meeting?
Click "Request a meeting" on a product page or the event schedule and leave your company name and contact details. No account is needed and it takes about a minute.
What happens after I apply?
Our coordinator contacts you to confirm your request. We then let you know the meeting time and place.
Do I deal directly with the supplier?
Yes. Quotes and detailed terms are negotiated directly with the supplier. MAKENOV supports the process — connection, communication and meeting coordination.
Which languages can I use?
You can talk in Korean, Vietnamese or English. Interpreters join the meetings so the conversation goes smoothly.
