Product details

Pontello Crispy is an air fryer equipped with a transparent heat-resistant glass container, highlighted by its innovative detachable heating head design.

The product’s key highlight lies in its seamless "Cook – Serve – Store" experience. After cooking, simply detach the heating head to use the glass bowl directly as tableware on the dining table, then snap on the lid to store any leftovers.

The product offers a 4.5L container suited for family needs and a 1.8L option ideal for small portions or 1–2 people. The transparent glass bowl allows for easy monitoring during cooking and is fully detachable for effortless dishwasher cleaning.

Pontello Crispy features 6 one-touch presets, including Air Fry, Reheat, Crisp, Bake (Bread/Cookies), Dehydrate, and Defrost.

With its compact design and the versatility to handle multiple steps in a single container, this product is perfectly suited for modern families, apartment dwellers, and anyone who values kitchen convenience.

About the brand

Daewon Industrial Co., Ltd is an enterprise established in Daegu, Korea since 1977. After nearly 50 years of operation in the manufacturing field, Daewon Industrial has accumulated rich experience from industrial machinery to products serving life. With the philosophy "Emotion in design, luxury in performance", the manufacturer wants to aim for products with minimalist designs, easy to use and suitable for modern living spaces. The brand focuses on balancing aesthetics and practical functionality, making household appliances more convenient in daily life.
