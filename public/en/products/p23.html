Product details

StepUp NEO is a next-generation passive back-support exoskeleton developed by FRT Robotics, South Korea, to reduce physical strain on workers performing repetitive lifting, bending, and material-handling tasks. Designed for manufacturing plants, warehouses, logistics centers, construction sites, and other physically demanding industrial environments, StepUp NEO combines a lightweight 3.6 kg structure with an ergonomic design. It allows workers to maintain natural movements, including walking, bending, and squatting, while helping reduce strain on the lower back during daily operations.

One of StepUp NEO's key advantages is its passive assistance technology powered by a gas spring mechanism, which operates entirely without batteries or electric motors. The device requires no charging and is not limited by battery life, allowing workers to access assistance whenever needed throughout their shifts. Its on-demand assistance system enables users to activate support when lifting heavy objects and deactivate it when walking or performing regular movements. With three assistance force options of 10, 12, and 14 kgf, StepUp NEO allows users to select the appropriate level of support for different working conditions while minimizing discomfort during extended wear.

Reducing lower-back muscle strain is another major advantage of StepUp NEO. According to electromyography (EMG) test data published by FRT Robotics, the device demonstrated an average reduction of approximately 43% in lower-back muscle fatigue under the manufacturer's testing conditions. Its lightweight construction, adjustable straps, and three available sizes (S, M, and L) accommodate a wide range of body types. Once properly adjusted, the device can be quickly put on and removed, making it convenient for everyday industrial use. These features make StepUp NEO a practical solution for businesses seeking to improve working conditions, support employee well-being, and reduce physical strain in jobs involving repetitive lifting and material handling.

About the brand

FRT Robotics is a Korean technology enterprise founded in 2015 by a research team with experience in the field of industrial robots and human motion support technology. The company has achieved many outstanding achievements such as the Good Design Korea 2022 Gold Award, being designated an innovative public procurement product in 2023 and participating in the Korean Government's DIPS 1000+ program. The StepUp product line has been deployed in many businesses and public agencies in Korea, including 360 devices supplied to firefighting and forestry units. FRT Robotics also actively introduces technology at international exhibitions such as MWC Barcelona, ​​Hannover Messe and GITEX Dubai, affirming its capacity in research, commercialization and international market development.
