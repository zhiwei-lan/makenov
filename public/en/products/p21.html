Product details

MyStarPick is an online voting platform from South Korea that leverages Blockchain and Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help beauty pageants, talent competitions, award ceremonies, and events conduct voting in a transparent, secure, and professional manner. The platform not only simplifies voting management but also enhances audience engagement and creates additional revenue opportunities for event organizers.

One of MyStarPick's key strengths is its ability to enhance voting transparency and prevent fraud. Blockchain technology enables secure data recording and verification, allowing voting results to be checked, while AI detects suspicious activities such as fake accounts, bots, and automated voting. This helps event organizers reduce the risk of vote manipulation, minimize disputes, and build greater trust among contestants and fans.

One of MyStarPick's standout features is its ability to combine free and paid voting. Fans can participate in daily voting or purchase additional votes to support their favorite contestants. Through its revenue-sharing model, event organizers can generate additional income from voting activities without having to invest in developing their own voting system.

The platform is also user-friendly and highly flexible in event management. Organizers can manage contestants, create multiple voting categories, customize voting rules, track votes, and choose how results are displayed. Participants can vote online through the website or mobile app. The system also supports payment management, revenue reconciliation, and NFT-based certification of voting results.

By combining transparency, anti-fraud technology, user-friendly management features, and a revenue-generating model, MyStarPick offers an ideal solution for organizers of competitions, events, award ceremonies, and brand voting campaigns looking to enhance their credibility, attract more participants, and maximize the value of audience engagement.

About the brand

MyStarChain is a Korean technology enterprise, developing MyStarPick - an online voting platform using Blockchain and AI. To date, MyStarPick has been used in more than 70 contests, attracting more than 180,000 users and recording more than 2.8 million votes. One of MyStarPick's most outstanding achievements is participating in voting for Miss Korea - a famous beauty contest in Korea. In addition, the platform is also used at international shows such as Best Model of Universe 2026 in Spain, PC.com Readers' Choice Awards and BusinessToday Industry Choice Awards in Malaysia, confirming its applicability in many different types of events and markets. MyStarChain also owns technology patents in Korea and was selected to introduce the solution at the InnoAsia program of the 2026 Busan International Film Festival. Since 2025, MyStarChain has actively expanded its operations in Vietnam, signed a cooperation MOU with GLOBAL PR HUB JSC and registered a patent for anti-voting fraud technology. In 2026, the business participates in InnoEX and K-Brand Expo in Ho Chi Minh City, signing 5 MOUs with local partners and cooperating with Victory Incubator Center. MyStarChain is also discussing implementing voting solutions with Miss Charm Vietnam, C-Laps and NSSC in the fields of entertainment, events and consumer surveys. MyStarPick currently supports voting interface in Vietnamese and displays prices in VND, and is integrating domestic payment methods such as ZaloPay and MoMo. Target customers in Vietnam include contest organizers, event and marketing companies, media agencies and marketing departments of businesses.
