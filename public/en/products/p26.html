Product details

Hydroponic strawberry cultivation requires more than just controlling water and nutrients. Growers also face challenges such as broken fruit stems, moisture buildup, poor drainage, and labor-intensive crop care and harvesting. An unsuitable growing bench system can negatively affect plant development and overall farm efficiency.

To address these challenges, WATS has developed two specialized hydroponic growing bench systems from South Korea: U HIGH BED KIT and T BED KIT. Both feature a single-support-post structure that elevates growing beds above the ground, optimizes space utilization, and simplifies installation and crop maintenance.

U HIGH BED KIT is primarily designed for strawberry cultivation. It consists of the U HIGH BED support structure, WING accessories that support fruit stems and clusters to help prevent breakage, and detachable POT trays for propagating strawberry seedlings from runners. This allows growers to use the same system for both commercial strawberry production and seedling propagation, saving space and reducing additional infrastructure investment.

Meanwhile, T BED KIT offers greater flexibility for cultivating strawberries, tomatoes, cucumbers, and bell peppers. The system is available in two configurations: STYROFOAM, which uses specialized foam growing troughs, and BRIDGE, which features support structures for substrate grow bags. The BRIDGE design minimizes contact with the grow bags, supporting better drainage and root-zone ventilation. Its structure is also designed to reduce heat absorption associated with metal growing troughs, helping maintain more stable root-zone temperatures and favorable growing conditions.

Both systems feature simple, modular assembly and can incorporate locally available materials to optimize installation costs. For greenhouse construction and irrigation companies in Vietnam, these solutions offer opportunities to expand their service portfolios, implement projects flexibly according to customers’ budgets, and enhance the long-term investment value of agricultural facilities.

About the brand

Originating from the WIGRO brand established in 2014, WATS is a Korean enterprise specializing in developing modern hydroponic farming equipment and solutions. In 2020, WATS officially established an independent legal entity, inheriting WIGRO's technology platform and experience in developing agricultural equipment, aiming to improve production efficiency and modernize farming activities. With experience exporting to Mongolia, Philippines, Japan, Hong Kong and the US, WATS has implemented many strawberry growing projects in Korea and internationally, notably the Philippines, Japan and Lao Cai (Vietnam). In Vietnam, WATS aims to have long-term cooperation with businesses that construct greenhouses, irrigation systems and high-tech agricultural equipment. With the ability to provide core components from Korea, design support, technical consulting and technology transfer, WATS provides opportunities for Vietnamese partners to expand their solution portfolio, proactively implement projects and develop the domestic hydroponic market.
