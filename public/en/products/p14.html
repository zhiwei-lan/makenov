Product details

HELLO SLEEP offers a wide range of Made in Korea bedding products featuring premium materials, modern designs, and advanced fabric processing technology, with a focus on softness, coziness, and comfort. By carefully researching the combination of fabrics and filling materials, the brand develops bedding collections with distinctive characteristics to meet diverse consumer needs and preferences.

Among its standout products is Castella, one of HELLO SLEEP's best-selling comforter collections, known for its soft fabric, cozy filling, and variety of designs. The brand also offers Tencel Modal bedding, made with premium Lenzing fibers for a silky-smooth feel; Hotel Room Cotton bedding, crafted from high-density 60s cotton for hotel-inspired comfort and luxury; and the Ice Pudding Modal × Duralon comforter, which combines Modal with cooling fabric technology to provide a refreshing, comfortable feel against the skin in warm weather.

HELLO SLEEP also develops functional bedding collections, including Don't Worry M2, made with ultra-fine microfiber designed to help reduce exposure to house dust mites, and Refresh Collagen, featuring materials promoted for their antibacterial and deodorizing properties. With a wide variety of materials, designs, colors, and sizes, HELLO SLEEP offers diverse options for distributors seeking high-quality Korean bedding products to expand their product portfolios in Vietnam.

In addition to its existing product collections, HELLO SLEEP offers OEM/ODM manufacturing services directly from its own factory in South Korea, supporting customized bedding production in terms of materials, designs, colors, sizes, and product specifications. This creates a valuable opportunity for Vietnamese distributors, retail chains, and brands looking to develop exclusive product lines or establish their own bedding brands with Made in Korea manufacturing.

About the brand

HELLO SLEEP is a bedding brand belonging to Blanc Corporation, established in 2020 in Daegu, Korea. With the philosophy "Comfortable sleep creates a good day", HELLO SLEEP focuses on developing high-quality bedding products, combining selected materials, modern design and Korean manufacturing technology. The brand owns its own factory in Korea, proactively from material research, design to production, and provides OEM/ODM services upon request. HELLO SLEEP is currently available on major e-commerce platforms such as Ohouse, Coupang, Naver Smart Store and 29CM, with many products well received by Korean consumers. According to corporate records, the brand records approximately 208,000 sales per year and annual revenue of 15.5 billion KRW. With direct production capacity, business experience in Korea and a diverse product portfolio, HELLO SLEEP is looking for distributors and OEM/ODM partners in Vietnam to expand the market and develop long-term cooperation.
