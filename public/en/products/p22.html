Product details

SAPYUNG is a Korean food brand owned by AGNI Co., Ltd., originating from a small restaurant in Daegu in 2020. The brand specializes in developing ready-to-eat curry and sauce products, combining carefully selected ingredients, meticulous cooking methods, and everyday convenience.

One of SAPYUNG's signature products is its Low-Moisture Keema Curry, made with a generous amount of Korean onions slowly sautéed to develop natural sweetness, rich flavor, and a thick, hearty texture. Its flagship Keema Curry contains approximately 70% onions, creating a distinctive consistency and taste compared to conventional curry products. Combined with minced meat, vegetables, and a carefully balanced blend of spices, the curry delivers a savory, well-rounded flavor and a satisfying texture that sets it apart from traditional liquid curry sauces.

SAPYUNG offers a diverse product lineup, including Original Keema Curry, Spicy Keema Curry, San Marzano Tomato Curry, Korean Black Bean Sauce, Matcha Curry, and Mala Curry. Each variety offers its own distinctive flavor profile, ranging from the gentle sweetness of onions and the refreshing tanginess of tomatoes to the smooth, creamy notes of matcha and the aromatic spiciness of mala, catering to a wide range of consumer preferences.

SAPYUNG's products are fully prepared and easy to heat and serve with rice, noodles, or a variety of other dishes. In addition to its frozen product range, the brand also offers retort curry products that can be stored at room temperature, providing greater convenience for storage and distribution. With its distinctive flavors, innovative product portfolio, and convenient preparation, SAPYUNG is pursuing international market expansion and seeking importers and distribution partners in Vietnam.

About the brand

AGNI Co., Ltd. is a Korean food enterprise headquartered in Daegu, established in June 2024, operating in the field of development, sales and distribution of processed foods, owning the SAPYUNG brand. Inheriting experience from the restaurant model started in 2020, AGNI focuses on building high-end convenient food products, focusing on ingredient quality, processing methods and consumer experience. AGNI's strength lies in its ability to develop differentiated products, combining culinary experience with modern processing and preservation technology. The business continuously expands its product portfolio, developing food lines that suit convenient consumption trends. AGNI's products are announced to be manufactured at facilities that meet HACCP, FSSC 22000 and ISO standards, demonstrating the orientation of focusing on quality and food safety. AGNI recognizes progress in business operations and distribution expansion in Korea. According to NICE data published on JobKorea, the company's 2025 revenue will reach about 553 million KRW, an increase of about 151% compared to 2024. The SAPYUNG brand is already present on retail channels such as Market Kurly and Ananti, and officially joined Twelve Market under House of Shinsegae Cheongdam in August 2026. With a product development platform and expanding distribution network, AGNI aims to find import and distribution partners to bring its products to the Vietnamese market.
