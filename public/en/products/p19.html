Product details

HIPER HEALD is a line of engineering plastic healds (Plastic Healds) developed and manufactured by NEGENTEC, a South Korean company established in 2001. As an essential component of industrial weaving machines, healds guide and control the movement of warp yarns, enabling them to interlace precisely with weft yarns to form fabric. Despite their small size, healds play a crucial role in maintaining production stability, minimizing yarn breakage, ensuring fabric surface quality, and supporting efficient weaving machine operation.

The key feature of HIPER HEALD is its use of engineering plastic materials as an alternative to traditional steel healds. NEGENTEC positions itself as a pioneer in developing and commercializing this technology, with the capability to injection-mold ultra-thin plastic components measuring 0.4 mm or less in thickness. This manufacturing technology requires high precision to produce lightweight, ultra-thin components that can withstand the demands of continuous operation in industrial weaving environments.

Compared to traditional metal healds, HIPER HEALD offers the advantage of a lightweight design, helping reduce the mass of moving components during the weaving process. In addition, its yarn-guiding eye design and surface properties are engineered to minimize friction, reduce yarn damage and breakage, particularly when producing lightweight fabrics with fine yarns. According to the manufacturer, the product also offers excellent wear resistance and a long service life, helping reduce the frequency of component replacement and maintain stable weaving machine operation.

HIPER HEALD is available in various specifications to meet different weaving requirements, including lengths of 280 mm, 302 mm, and 330 mm, with a standard packaging quantity of 10,000 pieces per box. The product is designed for fabric weaving mills and textile machinery parts suppliers seeking lightweight, precise, and reliable components that help improve operational efficiency and maintain consistent quality control throughout the production process.

About the brand

NEGENTEC is a Korean enterprise established in 2001 in Daegu, specializing in researching and manufacturing industrial textile machine spare parts. With more than 20 years of experience, the company stands out in the field of developing technical plastic woven goggles under the HIPER HEALD brand, owning many intellectual property registrations related to technology and product design. NEGENTEC products have been exported to China, Malaysia, Vietnam, India and many other markets. Currently, the business is expanding its distribution network in Vietnam, looking for long-term cooperation partners in the field of textile machine spare parts.
