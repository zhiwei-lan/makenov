Product details

In the textile industry, detecting defects on fabric surfaces before production or shipment plays a crucial role in quality control. However, many inspection processes still rely on manual visual checks, which are prone to human fatigue, making it difficult to maintain consistent inspection quality and often lacking visual records for reference when customer complaints or quality disputes arise.

To address these challenges, AutoLabs, a technology company from South Korea, has developed AutoVision & AutoBrics, an integrated solution that combines material inspection hardware with an artificial intelligence (AI) platform to detect anomalies on fabric surfaces, record inspection results, and digitalize quality management processes.

A key highlight of the solution is its AI technology, which can learn from images of defect-free fabric to identify anomalies without requiring a large dataset of defects in advance. Combined with a modular hardware design, the system can be configured for integration into existing fabric inspection lines, allowing manufacturers to adopt smart inspection technology without necessarily replacing their entire machinery setup.

AutoVision – Smart Fabric Inspection Hardware System: Integrates industrial cameras, lighting systems, and an on-site processing computer to capture images of material surfaces during operation. Its modular design allows the system configuration to be customized according to production line specifications and actual inspection conditions.

AutoBrics – AI-Powered Defect Detection and Management Software Platform: Analyzes images captured by AutoVision to highlight areas with suspected defects, supports operators in verifying inspection results, and stores inspection images and data. The software also generates quality reports for individual fabric rolls, enabling manufacturers to trace inspection records, manage quality control, and provide inspection documentation to customers and importing partners.

AutoVision and AutoBrics work seamlessly together to form a comprehensive fabric inspection and quality management system. AutoVision captures images of fabric surfaces directly on the production line, while AutoBrics analyzes and records the data, connecting the entire workflow from on-site inspection to the generation of quality reports for buyers.
