Product details

The Dry Calendering Machine for secondary batteries is specialized equipment designed to form electrode sheets and control electrode thickness and density through the calendering process, supporting research, development, and testing of dry electrode technology.

Unlike the wet electrode process, which uses solvents and requires a drying stage, the dry electrode process does not use solvents during electrode formation. Eliminating solvents and the drying stage simplifies the manufacturing process and has the potential to reduce production time, energy consumption, and operating costs.

Depending on the scale and application requirements, A.SYS Technology offers two main Dry Calendering Machine models: DCM 120-3 and DCM 300-5.

The DCM 120-3 Dry Calendering Machine features three horizontally arranged rollers, making it suitable for small-scale material research, development, and process testing. It supports a maximum material width of 100 mm, a maximum pressing force of 20 tons, and roller precision within 2 µm.

The DCM 300-5 Dry Calendering Machine features five rollers, integrating sheet forming, calendering, and lamination into a single system. It supports a maximum material width of 300 mm, a maximum pressing force of 60 tons, and higher operating speeds, making it suitable for large-scale process testing and pilot production line development.

The equipment is designed for battery manufacturers, companies developing dry electrode technology, research institutes, and laboratories specializing in battery materials and energy. A.SYS Technology holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 certifications, along with four patents related to dry electrode processes. The company also has the capability to design and manufacture customized equipment based on customers' technical specifications and specific requirements. Businesses interested in dry electrode manufacturing equipment are welcome to contact A.SYS Technology to discuss technical specifications, customized design solutions, and distribution partnership opportunities.
