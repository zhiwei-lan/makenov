About the company

INCORE (founded in 2014 in Daegu, Korea) is a medical device manufacturer, notable for its disposable endoscopic devices, HEMOBLOCK chitosan hemostatic dressings and HYLACELL wound dressings. Based on research experience in the field of medical devices and skin care, INCORE developed MIRALET — a dermacosmetic line with products containing plant PDRN, plant exosomes and hyaluronic acid.
