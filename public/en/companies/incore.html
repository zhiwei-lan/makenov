About

INCORE (founded 2014, Daegu) develops and manufactures chitosan-based medical devices — notably the HEMOBLOCK hemostatic dressing and HYLACELL, a hydrogel wound dressing prescribed by dermatologists. MIRALET is its dermacosmetic line built on the same formulation work, delivering plant-based PDRN and plant exosomes in daily-use skincare.
