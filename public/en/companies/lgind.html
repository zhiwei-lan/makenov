About

LARGE Co., Ltd. (founded Dec 2009, Daegu) manufactures automotive components centred on glass-fibre textiles, thermoplastic composites and exhaust-system insulation. It holds IATF 16949:2016, established an in-house research institute in 2010, and signed a joint composite development agreement with Germany's Fraunhofer ICT in 2016. Its FIRESSAK brand applies that heat-resistant material base to EV fire blankets, with supply records to public bodies including the Gyeongnam Fire Department and Seongnam Urban Development Corp.
