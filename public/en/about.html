Limitations of existing sourcing

It's easy to miss out on new products at trade shows alone.

Fixed timelines, high costs, and complex follow-up communications slow down global sourcing.

01

Can only be found during a limited period of time

Products and suppliers are only available during the few days of the fair. If the schedule doesn't work out, you'll have to wait for the next opportunity.

02

Every time you source you incur a cost

There are recurring costs such as flights, accommodations, and interpretation. Comparing multiple products takes more time and costs more.

03

Connection after consultation is difficult

You will need to reorganize the business cards and materials you received on site and contact them. It also takes time to check quotes and transaction terms.

How to use it

From membership registration to supplier inquiry, 4 steps

STEP 01

Sign up

Select your country and enter your company and contact information.

STEP 02

Verify your business

In Vietnam, authentication is done by tax code, in Korea, by business registration number, and in other countries, by company domain email.

STEP 03

Check transaction conditions

Once authentication is complete, you can check the price, minimum order quantity (MOQ), delivery date, and supply conditions and save it as a product of interest.

STEP 04

Enquire or request a meeting

Select product inquiry, quotation request, or video consultation to request a direct request from the supplier.

Frequently asked

Before you sign up

Does it cost anything?
Signing up, verifying, browsing and requesting quotes are all free. No membership fee and no commission.
Why does seeing prices require verification?
It is the condition on which manufacturers publish real prices. If anyone could see them, they would not post them.
Can I order a small quantity first?
It depends on the product. Those tagged negotiable can discuss smaller orders, so state your target quantity when you enquire.
Do I pay through MAKENOV?
No. Requests go straight to the manufacturer, and you contract and pay with them. We add no margin.
Can I get documents for import registration?
Select the documents you need on the quote form: ingredient list, certificate of origin, test report and catalogue.
Which countries are the products from?
Manufacturers worldwide can list. Origin is shown on each product, and the catalogue keeps growing.

Verify now and see the pricing

Verification is free and takes about a minute. You are welcome to browse first.
