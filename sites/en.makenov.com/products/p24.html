Product details

JIMBO is a series of smart transport robots designed to assist with moving goods, materials, and equipment across various environments, including manufacturing plants, warehouses, industrial facilities, farms, hotels, hospitals, and golf courses. With the ability to automatically follow users, JIMBO provides flexible transport solutions that reduce manual pushing and pulling while helping optimize operational workflows.

One of JIMBO's standout features is its Follow-Me Technology, which uses Ultra-Wideband (UWB) wireless positioning to determine relative positions and automatically follow a designated user. The robots are also equipped with sensors such as LiDAR, ultrasonic sensors, and Inertial Measurement Units (IMU), supporting obstacle detection, movement adjustment, and enhanced operational safety. Depending on the model, the robots can operate in follow-me mode, manual mode, or autonomous mode along predefined routes. This allows employees to focus on their primary tasks while the robots assist in carrying necessary goods and equipment, offering significant advantages for jobs that require frequent movement between different locations in factories, warehouses, or service facilities.

Another key advantage is JIMBO's flexible design and customization capabilities. The robotic system can be integrated with various frames, cargo containers, and specialized transport equipment. In particular, its modular technology enables the possibility of upgrading certain conventional carts by integrating drive units and sensors, allowing businesses to utilize existing equipment without necessarily replacing their entire transport systems.

JIMBO offers a diverse range of robot models designed for different applications. Jimbo B1 specializes in transporting boxes, components, and small materials within factories and warehouses. Its compact base allows multiple containers to be stacked and transported simultaneously, while supporting automatic user-following and convenient battery replacement. Meanwhile, Jimbo B6 features a design similar to a conventional industrial utility cart, combining automatic user-following with manual operation, even when the battery is depleted. It is well suited for transporting goods, raw materials, and equipment across production areas and warehouses.

Jimbo C1 is specifically designed for cleaning and waste collection tasks, featuring a frame that holds garbage bags and sacks while accommodating cleaning tools such as brooms and dustpans. The robot automatically follows cleaning staff during operation, reducing the need to manually push or pull waste collection carts. Its foldable holder also allows convenient removal and replacement of full waste bags. In contrast, Jimbo C2 is an autonomous transport robot capable of traveling along predefined routes, making it suitable for repetitive transport tasks. It features a closable cargo compartment that keeps items organized and helps limit their exposure to the surrounding environment during transportation.

For golf course applications, Jimbo G1 and Jimbo G3 are designed to automatically transport golf bags while following players around the course. Jimbo G1 features a sturdy frame and a front-wheel assembly capable of rotating 180 degrees, enabling flexible direction changes and convenient manual handling when needed. Jimbo G3 stands out with its foldable trolley design and detachable drive unit, making it easier to transport, store, and operate. Both models aim to reduce the effort required to push or pull golf bags, providing golfers with a more comfortable and convenient experience on the course.

About the brand

JIMBO is the smart transportation robot brand of Jimbo Robotics, a Korean technology enterprise founded in 2020 in Daegu, specializing in researching and developing automatic robot solutions that follow people. Developed on a technology platform from Daegu Gyeongbuk Institute of Science and Technology (DGIST), JIMBO stands out with wireless positioning and motion control technology, allowing flexible robot applications in production, logistics, agriculture and services. To date, Jimbo Robotics owns 8 patents in Korea, 1 patent in the US and has 2 international patent filings under the PCT system, demonstrating investment in core technology research and development. The brand has also introduced robot lines at major Korean technology exhibitions such as FIX 2024 and Gwangju Future Industry Expo 2024. With a human-centered robot development orientation, JIMBO aims to popularize automation solutions that are affordable, easy to apply and can be customized according to actual needs, gradually realizing the vision of "one robot for each person".
