Product details

Marienne’s large-size handkerchief is the brand’s flagship product, designed not only as a practical everyday handkerchief but also as a versatile fashion accessory.

The product measures 54 × 54 cm, making it larger than traditional small handkerchiefs, and is made from a blend of 60% cotton and 40% rayon. Its key features include a delicately finished picot edge, a wide range of colors, and modern patterns. The handkerchief can be used in various ways—as a handkerchief, bandana, petite scarf, hair accessory, bag accessory, or tumbler wrap. This versatility allows Marienne to appeal not only to the traditional handkerchief market but also to fashion- and lifestyle-oriented consumers.

Thanks to carefully controlled weaving and fabric-finishing techniques, the handkerchief maintains a soft texture while minimizing deformation and fraying even after repeated washing. Its good absorbency and relatively quick-drying properties make it well suited to Vietnam’s hot and humid climate as well as frequent daily use on the go. Especially during summer in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, or for outdoor activities, its larger size offers greater practicality than conventional small handkerchiefs.

Marienne also has proven export experience in Vietnam. Its large-size handkerchiefs are currently distributed in the Vietnamese market and have received positive feedback, with repeat orders continuing to follow.

In addition to its existing product lines, Marienne also offers OEM/ODM and custom production services, making it suitable for distributors, select shops, fashion brands, and businesses seeking gift items or textile products with their own designs.

In addition to handkerchiefs, Sunmion also develops a range of fabric products such as cushion covers and pajama pants. These products have received positive feedback at pop-up events in Korean department stores and are also being considered as part of the company’s plans to expand its distribution portfolio in the Vietnamese market.

About the brand

Marienne is a brand of handkerchiefs and fabric accessories developed by Sunmion Company, a Korean enterprise that inherits the handkerchief business foundation that has been maintained for three generations since 1953. Starting from the grandmother's generation, the family has been involved in the handkerchief industry for more than 70 years, accumulating experience in material selection, production and product finishing. Based on that long-standing foundation, Sunmion developed Marienne in a more modern direction, turning the handkerchief from a familiar item into a product that is both practical and can be used as a fashion accessory. Currently, Sunmion is expanding Marienne to the international market. In Vietnam, the large size handkerchief line has been officially exported, put into actual distribution and continues to receive additional orders.
