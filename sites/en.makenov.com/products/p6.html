Product details

Nightswimming Nail Perfume is a unique Korean beauty product that brings together two needs usually treated separately in a single product: caring for the nail area and enjoying fragrance like a solid perfume.

Unlike conventional perfumes that are sprayed onto the body, this product comes in a compact 10g balm format designed to be applied directly to the nails, the skin around the nails, and the fingertips. As it comes into contact with body heat, the balm gradually softens, helping moisturize the dry-prone skin around the nails while gently releasing fragrance from the fingertips.

The formula contains three plant-based oils—organic camellia oil, almond oil, and coconut oil—designed to help care for and soften the skin around the nails while maintaining a comfortable feel during use. The product is ethanol-free, offering a fragrance experience that differs from traditional spray perfumes.

The product is available in 8 fragrance options, ranging from delicate floral notes and refreshing citrus scents to warm woody aromas. The Signature Line is inspired by some of Korea’s well-known coastal destinations, including Busan, Geoje, Tongyeong, and Namhae. Its artistic packaging and distinctive “nail care + fragrance” concept set it apart from conventional solid perfumes and nail care products. The Special Line includes Gardenia Delight, Azalea Visle, Maeong Ho Bok Cho, and Maeong Ho Chul Rim, offering additional choices for consumers who enjoy a variety of fragrance styles.

Not only is it different in the way it is used, but Nail Perfume was also developed as a product with strong design value. The packaging for each fragrance features illustrations connected to the unique story behind the scent, making the product stand out easily in displays and well suited for gift purchases, lifestyle products, or design-oriented beauty collections.

Notably, within the scope of research on products currently available in the Vietnamese market, both solid perfumes and nail care products are already present. However, no product has been identified with a direct positioning similar to Nightswimming Nail Perfume, which combines nail care and fragrance in a single product. This distinction could allow distributors to build a new product story rather than competing directly with the solid perfume products that are already familiar to the market.

About the brand

Nightswimming is a Korean fragrance & lifestyle brand inspired by the sea, nature, and the distinctive regions of Korea. Rather than simply creating fragrances, the brand focuses on expressing the stories and emotions of each place through scent, offering moments of relaxation and time for oneself. One of Nightswimming’s defining characteristics is the combination of local stories, naturally derived ingredients, and emotionally engaging design. The brand’s fragrance lines are developed and tested through multiple rounds of collaboration with fragrance experts, creating a cohesive experience that connects the story, scent, and product design.
