Product details

HELIBEAN COFFEE is a Korean coffee and dessert café franchise concept featuring the signature “Coffee · Waffle · Bingsu” combination. Rather than focusing solely on traditional coffee beverages, the concept brings together coffee, bubble tea, fruit-based drinks, waffles, and bingsu in a single location. This combination caters to diverse customer needs, from daily coffee drinkers and customers seeking refreshing beverages to groups of friends and families looking to enjoy desserts together.

One of the key highlights of this café concept is its diverse menu, combining specialty coffee options such as Coconut Coffee and Swan Cream Latte with smoothies, frappés, and bubble tea. The menu also features waffles that are crispy on the outside and soft and chewy on the inside, served with fresh fruits and cream, alongside Korean-style bingsu topped with mango, strawberries, red beans, and other ingredients. In particular, the compact Cup Bingsu offers a convenient takeaway option for customers on the go.

For the Vietnamese market, the café concept also aims to adapt its menu to local consumer preferences. Products such as bubble tea, coconut coffee, fresh fruit waffles, and Cup Bingsu are presented as potential offerings tailored to Vietnamese customers. By combining coffee, refreshing beverages, and desserts within a single concept, HELIBEAN COFFEE provides business partners with greater flexibility to develop menus suited to different customer segments and local market characteristics.

HELIBEAN COFFEE also stands out for its flexible store formats, ranging from compact cafés of approximately 33 m² focused on quick service and takeaway to larger locations of 99 m² or more with dedicated seating areas for dine-in customers. Store designs can be adapted to different premises, allowing business partners to explore various operating models based on their locations and business needs.

By combining a diverse beverage menu, signature desserts, and flexible store formats, HELIBEAN COFFEE offers a café concept that brings multiple experiences together in one space. This business model is worth exploring for F&B companies in Vietnam looking to develop a coffee and dessert café concept rather than focusing solely on traditional coffee beverages.

About the brand

HELIBEAN COFFEE is a coffee and dessert brand belonging to HELIBEAN Co., Ltd., an enterprise established in 2017 in Daegu, Korea. With an operating background in the field of coffee production, roasting and distribution, HELIBEAN gradually developed from a coffee business into a coffee brand with a system of franchised stores in the Korean market. One of HELIBEAN's outstanding strengths is owning its own coffee roasting facility at its headquarters, proactively developing product lines such as Wonder Blending and Dutch coffee. Production capacity combined with store business activities creates a foundation for the brand to build a product portfolio, control the roasting process and develop coffee supply for the system. HELIBEAN COFFEE has registered for franchising in Korea since 2021 and developed its own branded stores. Currently, the business is targeting the Vietnamese market, looking for experienced F&B partners to exchange cooperation opportunities and develop Korean-style coffee and dessert brands.
