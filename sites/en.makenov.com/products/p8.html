Product details

ART&HUG’s mother-of-pearl Gat keyring combines Korean traditional culture, craftsmanship, and sustainable values. Inspired by the Gat, an iconic traditional Korean hat, ART&HUG reinterprets this familiar cultural symbol as a modern accessory that can be used on bags, pouches, or keys in everyday life.

What sets this product apart is the use of genuine mother-of-pearl and a 100% handcrafted production process. The surface changes in tone depending on the light, while each piece displays its own unique patterns and reflective effects. As a result, it feels less like a mass-produced keyring and more like a small handcrafted item with individuality and collectible appeal.

The story behind this product goes beyond simply reinterpreting a cultural symbol. ART&HUG develops products based on a K-Upcycling approach, using recycled materials to bring environmental and SDG-related messages into everyday items. Rather than presenting sustainability as an abstract concept, the brand turns it into a tangible experience that consumers can touch, use, and share as a gift.

For distributors, this product offers strong storytelling potential at the point of sale through its combination of Korean culture, handcrafted techniques, genuine mother-of-pearl, and eco-friendly values. Its compact and lightweight design also makes it easy to display and transport, making it suitable for souvenir shops, museum shops, lifestyle stores, gift retailers, corporate gifting, and K-culture distribution channels.

About the brand

ART&HUG is a Korean brand that develops cultural, gift, DIY and upcycling products. The brand combines traditional elements, modern design, craftsmanship and reused materials to create products that are both functional and have their own story. Besides aesthetic value, ART&HUG also focuses on messages about the environment, resource reuse and SDGs, thereby building a product portfolio suitable for gift shops, museum shops, lifestyle stores and the corporate gift market.
