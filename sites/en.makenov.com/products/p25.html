Product details

CMB (Carbon Master Batch) and FMB (Final Master Batch) are synthetic rubber compounds manufactured and supplied by JCT, a Korean company affiliated with Jinyang Oil Seal. These materials are used as raw materials for producing rubber components for the automotive, motorcycle, home appliance, and industrial machinery industries.

CMB (Carbon Master Batch): A rubber compound blended with reinforcing agents, fillers, and additives, without the addition of vulcanizing agents. CMB generally has a longer shelf life than FMB, making it more convenient for storage, inventory management, and subsequent manufacturing processes. CMB is JCT's primary product for production and distribution.

FMB (Final Master Batch): A rubber compound produced by adding vulcanizing agents and accelerators to CMB, making it ready for molding and vulcanization processes. FMB has a shorter shelf life than CMB and should be used within an appropriate period. JCT can manufacture and supply FMB upon customer request.

JCT's rubber compounds are available in various synthetic rubber materials, including EPDM, NBR, SBR, IIR, and ACM. The formulations can be customized to meet specific mechanical properties and performance requirements for different products.

CMB/FMB compounds are used to manufacture oil seals, gaskets, rubber hoses, vibration-damping components, and other technical rubber parts. A key advantage is the ability to develop customized rubber formulations based on customer requirements. JCT also supports the development of alternative material formulations to meet technical specifications while optimizing production costs.

These products are suitable for rubber component manufacturers, automotive and motorcycle parts suppliers, home appliance and industrial machinery manufacturers, as well as importers and distributors of synthetic rubber materials in Vietnam.

About the brand

JCT Co., Ltd. is a Korean enterprise established in 2011 in Daegu, specializing in formula research and production of CMB/FMB compound rubber on request. JCT is a member company of Jinyang Oil Seal, a unit with more than 30 years of experience in the field of manufacturing rubber components for the automotive, household appliance and industrial industries. In this production system, JCT is responsible for providing mixed rubber materials, while Jinyang Oil Seal and associated factories use raw materials to produce finished rubber components. In 2016, Jinyang Oil Seal established a legal entity in Vietnam with a production facility located in Dong Nai. With an experienced technical team, specialized mixing equipment system and quality control process using POP/MES, JCT is capable of developing rubber formulas that meet the technical requirements of each customer. After many years of providing raw materials to Jinyang Oil Seal's manufacturing factories, JCT is now expanding its business activities, looking for importers, distributors and partners to produce rubber components in the Vietnamese market.
