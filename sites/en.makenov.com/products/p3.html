Product details

Positioned between budget mass-market options and high-end luxury imports, HOONSUNG bridges the mid-segment market gap with high-quality, authentic MADE IN KOREA eyewear produced directly in Daegu, South Korea—offering clear origin, superior craftsmanship, and compelling unique selling propositions (USPs).

CHIMERIC features Swiss EMS TR-90 and French ARKEMA bio-plastic, select polarized lenses with UV400 (99.9% protection), and non-slip temple tips. Combining style with daily utility, it fills the mid-market gap in Vietnam for eyewear with certified origins and clear specs.

po·le optic specializes in full titanium and titanium-plastic hybrid frames. Featuring ultra-lightweight, corrosion-resistant builds, patented ultra-slim technology, and a registered trademark in Vietnam, it offers distributors a strong edge in the premium eyewear segment.

About the brand

HOONSUNG OPTICAL is an eyeglasses manufacturer in Daegu, Korea with more than 30 years of experience in the field of developing, manufacturing and exporting eyeglass frames. The company currently exports about 80% of its products to more than 50 countries, and owns its own brands such as CHIMERIC, po·le optic, FEEL SWISS and TEAM SPIRIT. HOONSUNG has control capacity from product development, design to production and quality management, along with a quality assurance certification system and more than 30 intellectual property rights. HOONSUNG also provides OEM/ODM allowing partners to customize frame design, material, size, color, plating, logo and packaging according to brand positioning and target market. This is a suitable advantage for importers, glass store chains and Vietnamese brands that want to develop their own product lines with direct production in Korea.
