Product details

KIOMER 3 is a wound care product developed using Endovision's proprietary KIOMER M3 technology, combining mushroom-derived chitosan with Atelocollagen (processed Type I collagen). Its formulation helps form a protective barrier, maintain a moist environment, and support the natural healing process of damaged skin. The product stands out for its application of a chitosan–collagen complex in wound care, along with its 3-FREE formulation: Free from preservatives, fragrances, and antibiotics. Developed in accordance with medical device standards, KIOMER 3 is designed for dermatology clinics, aesthetic centers, and professional wound care facilities.

RIBOHEAL và ALVION là hai dòng skin booster của Endovision, được phát triển nhằm đáp ứng các nhu cầu chăm sóc và cải thiện chất lượng làn da. RIBOHEAL kết hợp chitosan KIOMER M3 với PN (Polynucleotide), HA và công nghệ phức hợp collagen, hướng tới cải thiện độ ẩm, độ đàn hồi, kết cấu da và các dấu hiệu lão hóa. Điểm khác biệt nằm ở công nghệ tạo phức hợp sinh học nhằm hỗ trợ lưu giữ các thành phần trong công thức. Trong khi đó, ALVION là dòng mỹ phẩm skin booster kết hợp HA, chitosan và exosome, gồm ba phiên bản: WH hỗ trợ dưỡng sáng, WR chăm sóc nếp nhăn và A dành cho làn da dễ nổi mụn. Danh mục đa dạng giúp đáp ứng những nhu cầu chăm sóc da khác nhau trong lĩnh vực thẩm mỹ.

Lekio Recovery Flow is a beauty device featuring multi-frequency LDM ultrasound technology at 3 MHz, 10 MHz, and 19 MHz, designed to support skin elasticity, improve skin texture, and soothe the skin at home. The device offers three specialized treatment modes, a teardrop-shaped applicator head for precise care of smaller facial areas, and an ergonomic design for convenient use. It is paired with Lekio Gel Serum (120 ml), formulated with KIOMER M3 chitosan, niacinamide, D-panthenol, and aloe vera extract to help moisturize and soothe the skin while also serving as an ultrasound conductive gel. The key advantage of this duo is the combination of the device and serum in a simple, approximately 12-minute skincare routine, bringing advanced skincare technology into the comfort of home.

Lipodyn Gel is a hydrogel wound care product formulated with three key ingredients: Povidone-Iodine 3%, Chitosan, and Liposome Hydrogel. It is designed to protect damaged skin, help prevent wound contamination, and maintain a moist environment that supports the natural healing process. Its key advantage lies in combining an iodine-containing ingredient, protective chitosan, and a moisture-retaining hydrogel base in a single formulation. According to the manufacturer's instructions, Lipodyn Gel is suitable for caring for skin with a damaged protective barrier, wounds with low exudate, dry skin, and first-degree burns. The product is available in two tube sizes, 16 g and 51 g, offering convenience for different usage needs.

About the brand

Established in 2013, ENDOVISION is a Korean enterprise with more than 10 years of experience in research, development and production of medical devices, biomaterials and healthcare solutions. Possessing its own R&D system and production facility, Endovision has achieved quality management certifications such as KGMP, ISO 13485, has many technology patents and has signed an OEM supply contract with the global medical equipment group Zimmer Biomet. Outstanding in the company's research capabilities is KIOMER M3 - an exclusive patented biomaterial technology, using chitosan derived from mushrooms, with properties that help create protective films, maintain a moist environment and combine with collagen in wound care applications. Based on this technology foundation, Endovision continuously expands from specialized medical solutions to advanced skin care and cosmetic products, affirming its development orientation based on research capacity, exclusive technology and Korean medical production standards.
