Product details

C-Jet is a tool holder system developed by Top Tooling Systems, South Korea, featuring an integrated coolant delivery system that directs coolant precisely to the cutting zone. Unlike conventional tool holders, C-Jet not only securely holds cutting tools but also delivers coolant more effectively to the machining area, helping reduce heat, improve chip evacuation, and maintain stable cutting performance.

C-Jet's most distinctive feature is its ability to increase coolant pressure in proportion to spindle speed. According to the manufacturer's documentation, coolant pressure can increase by approximately 3 bar for every additional 1,000 rpm, reaching up to approximately 70 bar in certain configurations.

A single C-Jet system supports three coolant delivery modes: through-tool coolant delivery, direct coolant spraying around the tool holder, and mist spraying. In particular, C-Jet is designed to enable the use of cutting tools with internal coolant channels on certain CNC machines that do not have a built-in through-spindle coolant (TSC) system. This offers a significant advantage for manufacturers looking to upgrade their existing CNC machines without investing in entirely new equipment.

According to test data published by the manufacturer, C-Jet can help extend tool life, reduce machining time, and increase cutting speed, depending on machine conditions and workpiece materials. In addition to standard models, C-Jet specifications can be customized to meet customer requirements, allowing distributors to recommend suitable configurations for different CNC machines and machining conditions.

For distributors in Vietnam, the key value of C-Jet lies in its positioning as a machining performance upgrade solution rather than a conventional CNC accessory competing primarily on price. The product is well suited for distributors of tool holders, cutting tools, CNC machines, and machining solutions looking to expand their portfolios with a technologically differentiated product line. C-Jet offers opportunities to deliver greater value to manufacturing customers and adopt a solution-based sales approach instead of simply selling individual components.

About the brand

Top Tooling Systems is a Korean enterprise specializing in developing tool holders and coolant supply systems for CNC machining. The brand stands out with its exclusive C-Jet technology, which allows direct injection of coolant and increased pressure according to the rotation speed of the main shaft. C-Jet has been registered for patents in Korea and many foreign markets, including C-Jet mist spray technology with patent number 10-2303513. With a focus on its own technology, machining efficiency and the ability to customize according to customer needs, Top Tooling Systems offers CNC solutions with differentiation and high technical value.

C-Jet – Korean CNC Solution Featuring Patented Pressurized Direct-Injection Toolholder Technology

C-Jet is an advanced pressurized direct-cooling tool holder system that utilizes the rotational force of a machine tool spindle to increase coolant pressure and deliver coolant precisely to the cutting zone. By improving cooling efficiency and chip evacuation, C-Jet helps extend tool life, enhance machining quality, increase productivity, and reduce overall machining costs.
