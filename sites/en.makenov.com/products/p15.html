Product details

In the bedding industry, product quality is defined not only by design but also by materials, processing technologies, and manufacturing standards. Developed by Dongjin Bedding, a Korean bedding manufacturer with over 50 years of industry experience, the three product collections—Recolte, Iven, and Marive—combine AMB antimicrobial treatment technology with 100% modal fabric, offering a soft, comfortable sleeping experience with an emphasis on hygiene.

Recolte stands out for its 3M Thinsulate insulation filling, designed to provide excellent warmth while maintaining a lightweight and comfortable feel. Its classic botanical floral patterns and delicate lace details create a distinctive combination of advanced technology and elegant design. For distributors looking to expand into the premium thermal bedding segment, Recolte offers a compelling product with clear technological advantages and added value beyond aesthetics alone.

Iven stands out with its delicate floral patterns, elegant pink tones, and refined lace details, creating a warm and sophisticated bedroom atmosphere. A key highlight is its bedspread filled with 100% natural cotton, which can be used as both a decorative bed cover and a lightweight blanket, offering versatility and practicality. Iven is an attractive choice for distributors looking to develop a premium bedding collection that emphasizes elegant design, natural materials, and a refined bedroom aesthetic.

Marive features a minimalist design in two neutral shades, white and grey, making it easy to complement a wide range of modern interior styles. Beyond comforters, the collection includes matching bed pads and pillow covers, allowing customers to create a coordinated bedroom look. With its versatile neutral colors and complete product lineup, Marive offers distributors an attractive opportunity to reach a broad customer base with premium bedding that is easy to style and well-suited for coordinated set sales.

Backed by Korean manufacturing expertise, AMB antimicrobial treatment technology, and distinctive product features, Recolte, Iven, and Marive offer compelling options for Vietnamese distributors seeking high-quality premium bedding with technological differentiation and clear, marketable selling points.

About the brand

Established in 1970, Dongjin Bedding is a reputable bedding manufacturing enterprise in Korea, possessing an integrated production system from design, material processing to product completion. The business stands out with AMB antibacterial treatment technology, patent foundation in the US, Japan, China and Hong Kong, and quality certification records such as Woolmark, K-Mark and Q-Mark. Dongjin's brands are present in many major Korean retail systems such as Lotte, Hyundai and Shinsegae. With more than 50 years of experience, production capacity and its own technology, Dongjin Bedding provides Vietnamese distributors with a source of high-quality bedding products of Korean origin and a competitive advantage in quality and technology.
