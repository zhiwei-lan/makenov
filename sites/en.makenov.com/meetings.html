What's Different

Not the usual trade meeting

At a typical trade meeting, buyers arrive without knowing the products and leave with little more than business cards.

This event works the other way round. Products and company details are published first. You review them in advance, request meetings only with the suppliers you want, and get a scheduled 1:1 session with each.

Process

How it works

  1. 1

    Browse the suppliers

    Review the products and company details of the participating suppliers.

  2. 2

    Request a meeting

    Pick the suppliers you want to meet and leave your company name and contact details. Free, and it takes about a minute.

  3. 3

    Get your schedule

    Our coordinator contacts you and sets a time slot with each supplier.

  4. 4

    Meet 1:1 at the event

    On December 4 at Hotel Nikko Saigon, you meet each supplier one to one. Korean–Vietnamese interpreters are on hand.

FAQ

FAQ

Is there a fee to take part?

No. Requesting meetings and attending the event are both free.

Which companies can apply?

Distributors, importers, agents and any company in Vietnam looking to source Korean products.

How do I apply?

Click "Request a meeting" on the supplier you want to meet and enter your company name, industry, contact person and contact details. No account is needed and it takes about a minute.

What happens after I apply?

Our coordinator contacts you by the phone number or email you left to confirm your request and tell you the time slot with each supplier.

When is the deadline?

Requests are accepted until Sun, November 22, 2026. After that we fix the time slots and notify each company.

Can I take part if I don't speak Korean?

Yes. A professional Korean–Vietnamese interpreter joins every meeting.

Can I meet several suppliers on the same day?

Yes. Request each supplier you want to meet and we schedule the slots so they do not overlap.

What should I prepare for the meeting?

Bring business cards and a company profile. If you already have products in mind, prepare your questions on price, minimum order quantity and distribution terms.
