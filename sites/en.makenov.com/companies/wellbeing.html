About the company

WELLBEING HEALTH PHARM (Incheon, Korea) is a Korean cosmetics manufacturer with operating experience since 2007. The company develops its own brands such as Goeunbal, and also provides OEM/ODM services. Typical products are Goeunbal foot care cream containing urea, along with many skin and body care products. The company's products are distributed in Korea and have experience exporting to many international markets.
