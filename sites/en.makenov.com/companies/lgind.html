About the company

LARGE Co., Ltd. is an automotive materials and components manufacturer in Daegu, Korea, specializing in heat-resistant fiberglass fabrics, automotive heat and sound insulation materials, and thermoplastic composite materials. The company is certified to IATF 16949:2016, operates a corporate research institute and signed a composite technology development agreement with Fraunhofer ICT (Germany) in 2016. Based on the foundation of heat-resistant materials, LARGE developed the FIRESSAK brand specializing in fire safety solutions, notably fire blankets for vehicles and electric vehicles. FIRESSAK has supplied products to many agencies and businesses in Korea, including Gyeongnam Fire Department and Seongnam Urban Development Corporation.
